# Notion Knowledge

Use this skill for questions that require finding or comparing existing Notion knowledge.

1. Confirm the workspace, team, project, or page scope from the request.
2. Search before retrieving full pages. Keep broad searches bounded and refine ambiguous results.
3. Open the smallest set of relevant pages or database records and distinguish current evidence from stale or conflicting notes.
4. Answer with page titles and links when the MCP provides them. State when a claim is an inference.
5. Do not edit Notion during a research-only request.

Never invent a page, property, owner, or status that was not returned by the connected Notion MCP.
