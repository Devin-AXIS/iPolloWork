---
title: "Workflows"
description: "Use projects, pins, and archives to keep iPolloWork organized."
---

Workflows are how you turn normal iPolloWork chats into organized, repeatable work. Each project points to an independent folder and keeps its conversations, files, and configuration together. Switch projects from the sidebar, then rename, pin, archive, or delete conversations from their session menu.

You can also describe the workflow in the composer while you work. For example: `When this is done, rename the conversation to "Auth cleanup" and pin it`, or `Archive this conversation after the review is complete.` Those instructions map to iPolloWork's conversation controls: `rename` changes the title, `pin` keeps important conversations at the top, and `archive` hides finished conversations without deleting them.

Use separate projects when work needs a different folder, codebase, or configuration. Use pins and archives for lightweight organization inside a project, without creating a second hierarchy that competes with projects.
